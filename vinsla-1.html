✦ Our Story

Made by hand, given with love

Vinsla started as a small passion project — a kitchen table, a needle, and a lot of wool. What began as gifts for family quickly became something people wanted to keep and treasure.

Every animal at Vinsla is made entirely by hand, one at a time. We use natural, sustainable materials and take real pride in the details — from the curve of an ear to the texture of a tail.
