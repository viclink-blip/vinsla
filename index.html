✦ Our Story

Carved by hand, built to last

Vinsla started with a simple love for wood and wildlife. What began as a small craft became something people wanted to keep and treasure for generations.

Every animal at Vinsla is hand-carved from solid hardwood, one piece at a time. We take real pride in the details — from the curve of a horn to the texture of a mane.

✦ How We Work

From wood to your hands

Every Vinsla carving goes through the same careful process — no shortcuts, no machines.

🪵
Step 01

Select Wood

We choose quality hardwood for each piece — the grain and tone guide the final design.

🔨
Step 02

Carve

Each animal is shaped entirely by hand using traditional carving tools — slow, careful work.

✨
Step 03

Finish & Polish

Sanded smooth and finished with natural oil to bring out the wood's deep colour and texture.

📦
Step 04

Packed & Sent

Carefully wrapped and delivered to you — or straight to someone you love.
